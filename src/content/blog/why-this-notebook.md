Research moves quickly, but understanding rarely does. Papers accumulate in browser tabs; useful observations disappear into temporary documents; questions that felt important become difficult to reconstruct a month later.

This website is an attempt to slow that process down.

## What belongs here

I will use this space for three kinds of writing: paper notes, technical explanations, and research questions that remain unresolved. A note does not need to be a definitive tutorial. It should, however, distinguish clearly between what a paper claims, what the evidence shows, and what I currently believe.

## Writing as a test

Writing exposes gaps that passive reading can hide. If I cannot explain the role of a loss function, the assumptions behind an evaluation protocol, or why a representation should transfer, then my understanding is probably incomplete.

> The goal is not to publish frequently. The goal is to leave behind reasoning that remains useful.

## A living archive

Some notes will change as my understanding changes. Updates will be dated, uncertain interpretations will be marked, and unfinished thoughts will stay unfinished rather than being presented as conclusions.
