My paper notes use a deliberately simple structure. It is designed to make comparison easier when several papers use similar language but answer different questions.

## First pass: locate the claim

Before reading implementation details, I write down the problem definition, the central claim, and what would need to be true for that claim to matter.

## Second pass: follow the evidence

I map each important claim to an experiment, baseline, or ablation. Missing links become explicit questions rather than vague discomfort.

## Third pass: rebuild the idea

Finally, I try to restate the method without copying the authors’ framing. This is usually the point where hidden assumptions become visible.
